# Dragon Ball
## Dragon ball is a popular japanese anime and manga series created by Akira Toriyama. It follows the adventures of Goku, a brave and cheerful saiyan who trains to become stronger and protect the Earth from powerful enemies

## Main Characters
- Goku – The main character who loves fighting and training.
- Vegeta – A proud Saiyan prince and one of Goku's strongest rivals.
- Gohan – Goku's son who has incredible hidden power.
- Piccolo – A wise and powerful warrior who becomes an important ally.
- Bulma – A brilliant inventor who helps Goku on his adventures.
